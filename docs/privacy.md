# Playtest Recorder — Privacy Policy

Playtest Recorder is a desktop tool that records your own playtest sessions locally.
It does not collect analytics and has no server of its own.

## YouTube

When you choose to upload a session, Playtest Recorder uses the YouTube Data API
to upload the video file you selected to **your own** YouTube channel, with the
title and description you reviewed, as an **unlisted** video. It requests only
the `youtube.upload` permission, reads no data from your channel, and stores
nothing except the OAuth token on your own computer (`%APPDATA%\playtest-recorder`),
which you can delete at any time. Use of YouTube is subject to the
[YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and the
[Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). You can revoke the
tool's access at <https://myaccount.google.com/permissions>.

## Notion

If you publish a report to Notion, the notes and video link you reviewed are sent
to the Notion workspace you connected, using your own integration token.
