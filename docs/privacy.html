Playtest Recorder — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 August 2026

Playtest Recorder is a desktop tool that records your own playtest sessions locally. It does not collect analytics and has no server of its own.

YouTube API Services

Playtest Recorder uses YouTube API Services. When you choose to upload a session, it uses the YouTube Data API v3 to upload the video file you selected to your own YouTube channel, with the title and description you reviewed, as an unlisted video. It requests only the youtube.upload permission and reads no data from your channel.

By using this application you are agreeing to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of any data it receives is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What is stored, and where

Everything Playtest Recorder keeps stays on your own computer. There is no server, no account, and no transmission of your data to the developer.

  • OAuth token. After you sign in with Google, the access and refresh tokens are cached at %APPDATA%\playtest-recorder\youtube-token.json so you are not asked to sign in on every upload. Tokens are stored only on your machine and are never sent anywhere except to Google.
  • OAuth client credentials. Stored alongside it at %APPDATA%\playtest-recorder\youtube-client.json.
  • Recordings, notes and reports. Written to the session folder you chose on your own disk.

No YouTube user data is collected, stored, shared, sold, or used for advertising, analytics, or model training. Nothing is shared with any third party.

Data retention and deletion

Stored tokens are kept until you delete them or they expire, and they are refreshed only while you keep using the tool. You can remove all stored data at any time, and there is nothing held elsewhere that needs a request to erase:

  • Delete the stored tokens by deleting the folder %APPDATA%\playtest-recorder. The application holds no other copy.
  • Revoke the application's access to your Google account at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions (also reachable at https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions). Revoking immediately invalidates any token held on your machine.
  • Delete an uploaded video from YouTube Studio. Videos live on your own channel and are under your control, not the developer's.
  • Delete recordings and reports by deleting the session folder on your disk.

Because the developer never receives your data, there is no deletion request to make — but you may still contact us at the address below with any privacy question.

Notion

If you publish a report to Notion, the notes and video link you reviewed are sent to the Notion workspace you connected, using your own integration token. No YouTube user data beyond the video link you chose to publish is included.
