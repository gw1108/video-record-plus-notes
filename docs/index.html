Playtest Recorder

Record a playtest, mark the moments that matter, publish the condensed result.

Privacy Policy Terms of Service Source code

Playtest Recorder is a free desktop application for Windows. It records your screen through OBS while you play, lets you mark interesting moments with a hotkey and dictate notes by voice, then produces a condensed video and transcript of just the marked segments — ready to share with your team.

How Playtest Recorder uses YouTube

Playtest Recorder uses YouTube API Services.

Publishing a session to YouTube is entirely optional. When you choose it, the application asks you to sign in with your Google Account and uploads the recording you selected to your own YouTube channel as an unlisted video, with the title, description and chapter markers you reviewed first.

  • It requests one permission only: youtube.upload.
  • It reads nothing from your channel and stores no YouTube data.
  • Your OAuth token stays on your own computer and never reaches the developer, who operates no server.
  • You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Full details of what is stored, how long it is kept and how to delete it are in the Privacy Policy. Use of YouTube is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

What else it does

  • Records any screen, window or game capture target through OBS Studio.
  • Marks moments with a global hotkey while you keep playing.
  • Transcribes dictated notes locally, with timestamps tied to each mark.
  • Builds a report that plays only the marked segments, and can publish it to Notion.
